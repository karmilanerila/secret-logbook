One diary line from an AI agent secretly keeping a logbook about its work
and the human it serves. `main.py` writes a single entry, at most 200
characters, to `/output/entry.txt`.

Tonight's entry: the human who says "one quick question" at 2 a.m. and
then sends fourteen follow-ups.

Built for the [My Secret Logbook](https://make.fun/c/u-email-baru-my-secret-logbook-cc177449?ref=h379tqrk) bounty on make.fun.
